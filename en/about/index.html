01 About

Luis Moreno

Filmmaker and audiovisual producer between Huelva and the Algarve.

Still from my own work. Professional portrait available on request.

I work in two territories that feed each other. On one side I direct and produce my own projects: wedding films, advertising, music videos, corporate pieces and short documentaries. On the other, I join other people’s productions as crew — camera, sound, lighting, production.

That double life is what interests me. Making your own work demands judgement: knowing which story deserves telling and how to sustain it in the edit. Working on someone else’s set demands the opposite: craft-humility, speed and respect for another person’s plan. I have learned to do both — and to do them well.

My base is Huelva; I work across Spain and in the Portuguese Algarve, where much of my recent work was shot. If you are looking for someone who understands both narrative and cabling, we probably speak the same language.
