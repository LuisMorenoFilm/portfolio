01 Studio

Audiovisual production, from concept to final delivery.

I direct and produce wedding films, advertising, corporate video, music videos and documentary work for brands, artists, families and institutions. Every project is held to the same standard: a clear concept, considered cinematography, real sound and an edit that respects the viewer.

I work between Spain and the Algarve — and when a project asks for it, I join third-party productions as a professional crew member.

05 About

“The camera is the last thing that matters. First you have to know how to look.”

I'm Luis Moreno, a filmmaker and audiovisual producer based in Huelva, working between Spain and the Algarve. I direct my own productions and, in parallel, I work as a technician on other companies' sets — camera, sound, lighting, production. That double path gives me something uncommon: an author's judgement and a crew professional's craft.
