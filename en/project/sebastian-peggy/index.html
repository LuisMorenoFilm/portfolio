Sebastian & Peggy · 50th Anniversary

Family & CelebrationsTavira, Algarve · Portugal 2024

Fifty years of marriage, celebrated in Tavira with a surprise that was the real thing.

Half a century of shared life deserves a film. Sebastian and Peggy celebrated their 50th anniversary in Tavira with a surprise party organised by their family.

The challenge was to capture the reaction without ruining the surprise: a patient camera, a discreet long lens and an edit that lets gestures speak.
