01 Services

Professional services

Two ways to collaborate, one quality standard.

Full audiovisual production

I produce the entire project — or exactly the phases you need. I regularly work with brands, companies, artists, couples and institutions across Spain and Portugal.

01

Weddings & celebrations

Wedding films, anniversaries and family celebrations. Full coverage or cinematic summary, with authored direction and edit.

02

Advertising & brand content

Spots, campaigns and social content. Includes development, shoot and post. See the Tanqueray spec ad for the level.

03

Corporate & testimonial video

Internal and external communication, company films and testimonials with real people.

04

Music videos

Music videos and live recordings for artists and bands, from concept to colour master.

05

Documentary & storytelling

Short documentary pieces and brand narratives built with cinematic structure.

06

Events

Aftermovies, teasers and promotional pieces for events, venues and festivals.

For production companies

Freelance services for productions

If you represent a production company or a director, I can join your team in specific technical roles.

01

Camera operator / second camera

Set camera handling, with experience in advertising, music video and features.

02

Camera assistant

Support to the operator: lenses, focus pulling, kit prep.

03

Location sound

Dialogue and ambient recording on interviews, events and fiction sets.

04

Lighting / gaffer

Light rigging and operation, interiors and exteriors.

05

Production assistance

Shoot logistics, coordination and production support.

  • Camera operator
  • Second camera
  • Camera assistant
  • Location sound
  • Lighting / Gaffer
  • Production assistance

Available for shoots in Huelva, Seville, Andalusia, the rest of Spain and the Algarve.
